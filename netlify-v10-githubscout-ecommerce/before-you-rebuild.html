Why now is the right time

Rebuilding or redesigning

Every app you keep has to be set up again on the new theme. Every one you cut first is one less thing to pay a developer to move.

Hiring an agency

Walk into the first call knowing what you run. You will get a sharper quote, and you will spot it if anyone suggests adding a tool you already have.

Renewing a contract

Annual renewals lock you in. Check whether another app you already pay for covers the same job before you sign for another year.

Taking over a store

You inherited someone else's choices. Find out what is there and why before you own the bill.

What you hand to your team

The reviewed audit gives you a short, printable page you can forward to your developer, agency, or business partner. It lists the apps on your store, where two overlap, what we would cut and keep, and what to check before switching anything off.

We also ask about the tools that run behind the scenes and never show on your storefront: shipping, returns, accounting, inventory, help desk. You tell us what you use, and we flag anything that looks doubled up or that your new setup might replace.

We do not sell apps, we do not build stores, and we do not take referral fees. The advice has no angle.

A fair question

"Won't my agency do this?" Some will. Ask whether they check your existing apps before they quote, and whether they resell or partner with any of the apps they recommend. If the answers are yes and no, you may not need us.

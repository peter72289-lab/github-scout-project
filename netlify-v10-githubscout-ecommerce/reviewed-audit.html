How it works

1

Tell us about your store

A short form. Paste your app list from Shopify admin (we show you where), and add what you pay if you know it.

2

We read it properly

We run the store check, line it up against your list, and look for tools doing the same job or costing more than they should.

3

You get a one-page plan

What to cut, what to keep, what to replace, and in what order. Plus what to check before switching anything off.

What you get

  • Every app on your list, sorted into cut, keep, replace, or try first
  • Every overlap explained: which one we would keep, and why
  • Where Shopify itself already covers the job
  • A note on the tools that run behind the scenes, from what you tell us
  • Your numbers, not ours, wherever you give us what you pay
  • One round of follow-up questions by email

What you do not get

  • A pitch for another app. We do not sell apps or take commissions.
  • A subscription. You pay once.
  • A sales call, unless you ask for one.

Is it worth it for you?

Probably yes if

  • You run 15 or more apps
  • Nobody has reviewed the list in a year
  • You suspect doubles
  • A redesign, renewal, or new hire is coming

Probably not if

  • You run fewer than eight apps
  • You reviewed them recently
  • The free check found nothing

Not sure? Take the 2-minute quiz or run the free check first.

Questions

How do I find my app list?

In Shopify admin, go to Settings, then Apps and sales channels. Copy the names into the form. Screenshots are fine too if you would rather email them.

Do you need access to my store?

No. We never ask for a login or staff account. You send the list; we do the reading.

What do you do with what I send?

We use it only to write your plan. You can ask us to delete it at any time. See our privacy page.

When do I pay, and when do I get it?

After you send the form, we confirm the price and the delivery date by email. You pay only once you have agreed to both.

Privacy Policy

GitHub Scout collects the minimum information needed to run the ecommerce URL analyzer, process subscriptions, and follow up with operators who request a scan or agency pricing.

Information We Collect

  • Email address, storefront URL, monthly spend range, submitted notes, UTM parameters, landing page, and referrer.
  • Public HTML and public software signals from submitted storefront URLs for the purpose of generating recommendations.
  • Checkout and subscription metadata handled by Stripe. GitHub Scout does not store full card numbers.
  • Basic analytics events such as CTA clicks, form submissions, and thank-you page views.
  • Account data for signed-in users: email, hashed sign-in and session tokens, subscription status, saved scan reports, and monthly usage counts.

Cookies

We set one first-party, HttpOnly cookie (gs_session) to keep you signed in; it is strictly necessary and not used for advertising. The Meta advertising pixel is a third-party tracker and loads only after you accept the cookie banner. You can decline, and you can clear consent by clearing site data.

How We Use It

We use submitted information to generate storefront analysis, route leads and customers into follow-up workflows, improve the landing page and scanner, support customers, and maintain subscription records.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to operate the business — see the full list of subprocessors (Stripe, Supabase, Netlify, Resend, optionally Meta after consent, and, when we have one configured, a CRM webhook that receives your scan enquiry so we can follow up). We do not sell personal information.

Retention

Lead and customer records are kept as long as needed for support, billing, legal, and product improvement purposes. Storefront scan data may be retained to improve recommendations unless deletion is requested.

Your Choices

Signed-in users can export their data as a JSON file and delete their account and all associated data from the dashboard, with no request needed. You can also request correction, export, or deletion by contacting support through the Support page. Marketing emails include an unsubscribe option when email automation is active.

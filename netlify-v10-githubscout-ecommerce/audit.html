What we cannot

That second column is why the reviewed audit exists. You paste your real app list from Shopify admin, a person reads both, and you get a short plan: what to cut, what to keep, and what to do first.

Questions people ask

Can't I just look at my own app list?

You can, and you should. The list tells you what is installed. It does not tell you which ones overlap, which to cut first, or what to replace them with. That judgment is the part we do.

Will you try to sell me a different app?

No. We do not sell apps and we do not take a cut from the ones we mention. Our only product is the advice.

Are those prices what I pay?

No. They are public list prices for each app, so treat them as a rough guide. Your real bill can be higher or lower depending on your plan.

What if my store blocks the check?

Some bigger stores do. That is fine. The reviewed audit works from your own app list, so it does not need the check to get through.

Do I need a Shopify store?

Yes, for now. GitHub Scout only checks Shopify stores.

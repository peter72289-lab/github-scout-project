Questions

The things people ask first.

Can't I just look at my own app list?

You can, and you should. The list tells you what is installed. It does not tell you which ones overlap, which to cut first, or what to replace them with.

Will you try to sell me a different app?

No. We do not sell apps and we do not take a cut from the ones we mention. Our only product is the advice.

Are those prices what I pay?

No. They are public list prices, so treat them as a rough guide. Your real bill depends on your plan.

What if my store blocks the check?

Some bigger stores do. The reviewed audit works from your own app list, so it does not need the check to get through.

Do I need a Shopify store?

Yes, for now. GitHub Scout only checks Shopify stores.

Why it is hard to fix alone

Your admin shows a list. Not the answers.

Shopify shows every app you have installed. It does not show which ones do the same job, which one to keep, or what breaks if you switch one off.

So the list sits there. Cleaning it up feels like a project, and there is always something more urgent. Meanwhile the bills keep coming.

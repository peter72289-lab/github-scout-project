When did anyone last read your app bill?

Apps get installed one at a time, for a good reason, on a busy day. They almost never get removed. Here is a ten-minute way to find the ones you are paying twice for.

Here is how it usually happens.

You launch with a reviews app. A year later you want photo reviews, and the one that does them well is a different app, so you add it. You meant to cancel the first one. You did not.

Your email platform has its own signup forms. But before you set them up, someone installed a popup tool for a holiday sale. It is still there, still billing.

An agency added an upsell app during a site refresh. A freelancer added a second analytics tool to fix a tracking problem. A team member tried a loyalty app and moved on.

Nobody did anything wrong. Every one of those decisions made sense on the day. The problem is that nobody owns the list.

Apps get installed by whoever needs them. They get removed by no one.

Why it is hard to fix on your own

Your Shopify admin shows you every app you have installed. What it does not show you is which ones are doing the same job, which one to keep when two overlap, or what breaks if you switch one off.

So the list sits there. Cleaning it up feels like a project, and there is always something more urgent. Meanwhile the bills keep coming, and every extra app is one more thing loading on your pages.

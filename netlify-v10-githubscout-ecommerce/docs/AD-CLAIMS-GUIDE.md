# Ad & landing claims guide

Every claim in paid creative, landing copy, and email must be substantiable by
the product as built. This is both an FTC-risk control and the thing that keeps
refund/chargeback rates down. Existing ad images (`assets/operator-*-ad.png`)
predate the integrity fixes — regenerate them against this guide before spend.

## Substantiated — safe to claim
- "Detects the apps and third-party scripts running on a Shopify storefront across up to 10 live sources." (True; sources listed on /methodology.html.)
- "Flags overlapping paid tools in the same category and prices the consolidation." (True; overlap engine.)
- "Estimates savings from the apps we actually detect, using published-pricing benchmarks." (True — always pair the word *benchmark* with any dollar figure.)
- "Full report shows every detected app with the evidence trail behind each detection." (True; teaser vs full gating.)
- "Re-scan monthly and compare before/after." (True; dashboard compare.)

## Prohibited — do NOT claim
- Any specific dollar savings promise not tied to a completed scan of that store (e.g. "$21.6k–$78k/yr"). The old dropdown-derived figures are gone; don't reintroduce them in creative.
- "15 sources" as a present-tense capability. It is 10 live / 15 planned. Say "10 live sources" or "up to 10 sources."
- "Guaranteed savings," "cancel your apps and save $X," or any outcome guarantee.
- Naming a store's exact monthly bill for a tool ("you're paying $180 for Klaviyo") — we detect presence, not invoices. Say "benchmark cost."
- Implying affiliation with GitHub. (Also the rename issue — see LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.)

## Required qualifiers
- Any dollar figure in creative gets the word **benchmark** or **estimated** adjacent to it.
- Savings claims carry "based on apps detected in a scan; results vary."
- If a testimonial is used, it must be a real, permissioned customer (see below). No composite or illustrative testimonials.

## Testimonials
Use only real, dated, permissioned quotes from beta merchants. Store the
permission. The homepage has a testimonial section with placeholder slots
(commented out) — fill them only with verified quotes.

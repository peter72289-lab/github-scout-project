# Beta outreach kit

Goal: get 3–5 real Shopify merchants to run a scan and give a permissioned quote
before you spend on ads. Real proof is the single highest-leverage launch asset —
the product sells "we can audit your stack," so having audited nobody is the gap.

## Who to target
- Merchants doing $10k–$100k/mo (the core plan tier) with visibly heavy app stacks.
- Warm first: your network, Shopify community Slacks/Discords, agency contacts,
  people already complaining about app costs on X/Reddit.

## Offer
Free full audit (normally paywalled) in exchange for: permission to use an
anonymized before/after and, ideally, a one-line quote. No obligation to subscribe.

## Cold email / DM template
> Subject: Free audit of your Shopify app stack (looking for 5 beta stores)
>
> Hi {name} — I built a tool that scans a Shopify storefront and flags where the
> app stack overlaps or is overpriced, with the savings math shown from what it
> actually detects (no guessing). I'm lining up a handful of real stores to run
> it on before launch.
>
> Can I send you the full report for {store}? Takes me 2 minutes, costs you
> nothing. If it's useful, I'd love a one-line quote I can share (anonymized is
> fine). If not, no worries.
>
> — {you}

## Follow-up after sending the report
> Here's your report: {link}. The {N} things worth acting on first are at the
> top. Two asks, both optional: (1) did anything surprise you? (2) OK to share an
> anonymized version + a short quote? Happy to keep your store name out entirely.

## Turning results into proof
- Save explicit permission (a reply saying "yes, you can use this" is enough).
- Put quotes into the commented testimonial slots in index.html (only real ones).
- Turn one consented scan into an anonymized case study; link it near the sample report.
- Follow the AD-CLAIMS-GUIDE: no dollar promises you can't tie to a real scan.

## Tracking
Keep a simple sheet: store, contact, sent date, replied?, permission?, quote.
Five permissioned quotes clears the "proof" blocker in LAUNCH-READINESS.md.

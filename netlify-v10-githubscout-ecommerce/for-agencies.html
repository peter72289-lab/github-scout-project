Why not just use a free extension?

Free tools will list apps on a store, and some are good. Here is what we add, honestly:

  • How each app was spotted, so you can stand behind it in a proposal
  • A clear line between "clearly running" and "possibly running"
  • Tools doing the same job, grouped for you
  • No guessing: if we did not see it, we do not price it
  • We do not sell apps, so nothing in the result is steering you toward one

And what we do not have yet: no portfolio dashboard across clients, and no white-label report. If you need those, tell us. Early users shape what comes next.

What about stores that block the check?

Some larger stores block outside checks. When that happens, the result says so plainly, and the plan is built so a blocked check does not use up one of your checks.

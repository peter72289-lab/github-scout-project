Help and onboarding

Support

For billing, onboarding, storefront scan questions, refund requests, or agency pricing, contact the GitHub Scout team with the checkout email and storefront URL involved.

Launch Support Inbox

Email: support@githubscout.ai

Use this address for refund requests, scan follow-up, billing questions, account changes, and agency pricing.

What to Include

  • Your checkout email.
  • Your Shopify or ecommerce storefront URL.
  • Your plan: Operator, Director, or Agency.
  • The issue or decision you need help with.

Expected Response

During launch, support is handled manually, so we answer on business days (Monday to Friday), not around the clock. Aim for a first reply within one business day; anything that arrives over a weekend or a holiday is answered on the next one. If you have not heard back after two business days, reply to your own email to bump it — that has not been lost, it has been missed.

Priority goes to billing issues, failed checkouts, paid subscriber onboarding, and refund/cancellation requests. Refunds are approved or declined in that same first reply; once approved, Stripe usually takes 5-10 business days to put the money back on the card. See Refunds and Cancellation for what the guarantee covers.

Common questions

  • I paid but can't get in. Sign in at the login page with the exact email you paid with — your subscription is attached to it. We email a one-time link (check spam; it expires in 15 minutes).
  • My sign-in link didn't arrive. Request a new one; only the newest link works. If it still doesn't come, email support and we'll help.
  • What does a scan actually check? Up to 10 live sources — see the methodology. Dollar figures are published-pricing benchmarks, not your invoices.
  • Where are my scans? In your dashboard. Paid scans are saved automatically; use "Compare" to see before/after.
  • How do I cancel or delete my data? Cancel renewals via Stripe or support. To erase your account and all data, use the "Danger zone" in the dashboard. See Privacy.
